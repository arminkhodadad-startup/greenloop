Core capabilities

One platform, four connected value loops.

GreenLoop turns daily city behaviour into verifiable actions, rewards, merchant demand and municipal insight.

↗

Smart Mobility

Compare fastest, greenest and balanced routes across multiple transport modes.

  • Low-carbon route scoring
  • EV charger discovery
  • Trip verification and points
◎

Citizen Rewards

Reward verified sustainable behaviour with configurable Green Points.

  • Wallet and redemption
  • Sponsored challenges
  • Referral and streak systems
▥

Municipal Intelligence

Transform participation into actionable mobility, infrastructure and neighbourhood signals.

  • Issue reporting
  • EV site prioritisation
  • Campaign performance
◈

Green Commerce

Enable merchants to launch offers, verify QR redemptions and sponsor local missions.

  • Offer management
  • Customer conversion
  • Green certification
♧

Community Networks

Connect neighbourhoods, universities, companies and residents through shared missions.

  • Leaderboards
  • Academic communities
  • Neighbourhood campaigns
✦

AI Decision Support

Surface investment, participation and infrastructure opportunities from pilot data.

  • Green PropTech signals
  • Priority recommendations
  • Explainable scores
